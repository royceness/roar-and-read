Mission complete!

For grown-ups

Each mission is 6 questions. Every question ends on the right answer; what's tracked is whether it was right first try. First try moves an item up a step, a wrong tap moves it down one. Three steps up and it counts as known, and it then comes back now and then for review.

Journey is the step-by-step path: letter sounds first, short words as their letters are learned, then digraphs one at a time and a few sight words. New letters, digraphs and sight words are shown and said before they are asked, and brand-new items have two choices instead of three. In reading questions he can tap a letter to hear its sound; the answer still counts as right, but the item doesn't move up a step.

The kinds of question

🔤 First sound: hear a word, tap the letter it starts with; each letter says its sound when tapped.  🔊 Hear it: hear a word, tap the written word; a wrong word is read out before he tries again (sight words are always this kind).  📖 Read it: read a word silently and tap its picture.  🧩 Missing letters: hear a word and choose the missing digraph (sh, ch, ee…); each choice says its sound when tapped. Digraphs are shown in a highlight colour and their sound is played before the word after a right answer.  🔨 Build it: see a picture, hear the word and tap the letters in order; nothing is marked right or wrong until every box is full; then a wrong word turns red and he taps letters to take them back. Newer items get the easier kinds, and look-alike choices come in as an item moves up.
