# What the license covers

The MIT license in `LICENSE` covers the code and word lists in this repository.

Two kinds of file are included as-is and are not covered by it:

- **Speech recordings (`audio/`).** These were generated with ElevenLabs text-to-speech through fal.ai, and some were then cut down to single sounds. Their reuse is subject to those services' terms. You can regenerate them with your own account using `tools/make_audio.py`.
- **Icons (`icon.png`, `favicon.png`).** These are renderings of an emoji from Apple's emoji font. That artwork belongs to Apple. Replace the icons if that matters for your use.
